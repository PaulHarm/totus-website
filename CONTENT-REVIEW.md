# Source-content issues for review

These items were found in the existing website. They are not independently verified facts. Marketing copy in this redesign avoids the conflicting claims. The substantive imprint, privacy and terms text was retained from the source, with its available English translation, so the following needs review before replacing the live domain.

1. The imprint says HRB 288401 B, while the press fact sheet says HRB 234567. VAT ID DE123456789 appears to be a placeholder.
2. Existing legal pages are dated 12 November 2026, later than the 18 October 2026 review date. The redesign does not repeat that date in its page header.
3. The original contact phone was a placeholder. It has been omitted.
4. Tracker data-source counts differ between the homepage and product page (eight versus nine). The redesign describes multiple sources without a numeric claim.
5. PANI Hausverwaltung's stated status is pilot launch in preparation. Tracker is in internal use. These distinctions remain visible.
6. Original security/privacy descriptions differ on tenancy, TLS versions and EU-only processing versus a listed US subprocessor. The new security overview uses capabilities stated in the source and directs specific deployment questions to the security contact. Substantive original privacy/terms text remains for review.
7. ISO 27001 refers to hosting infrastructure; company certification was described as in preparation. SOC 2 was a roadmap item. No certification badge has been added.
8. Existing product screenshots are retained as product views, not used as customer endorsements. The newly constructed interactive walkthrough uses fictional property data and neutral labels.
9. The original demo scheduler had static availability and a mailto action. The redesign honestly prepares an email draft and offers a copy fallback; there is no live booking integration.
10. Careers pages describe role areas and invite a conversation about current openings. Specific salary and equity promises have not been carried forward.
11. The new website includes no fabricated customer logos, testimonials, investment performance or fundraising terms.
12. The available English imprint, privacy and terms translations still contain German paragraphs inherited from the source. Complete and approve these translations before public launch.
