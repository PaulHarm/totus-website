# PANI brand adaptation

Reference: https://www.pani-hv.de/ and https://www.pani-hv.de/reporting.html, viewed 19 October 2026. The user requested this brand's design and logo for the PANI product presentation.

- The original bear geometry comes from the `pani` symbol in https://www.pani-hv.de/assets/icons.svg?v=20260924a. The downloaded sprite is retained in source-content/pani-brand-icons.svg. `python prepare-pani-brand.py` extracts the standalone mark without altering the paths.
- Fraunces and Plex webfonts come from https://www.pani-hv.de/assets/fonts/fraunces-latin.woff2 and https://www.pani-hv.de/assets/fonts/plex-latin.woff2.
- PANI green #254b3e, deep green #19382d, white, pale sage surfaces, serif headings and the divided bear/wordmark composition follow the reference site.
- Both German and English PANI Hausverwaltung and PANI Tracker pages use the brand theme. Product cards, strip and navigation use the same bear. Tracker is presented as a PANI product with a Tracker sublabel.
- Both product heroes include a keyboard-operable three-view software design preview in the PANI identity. These previews are clearly labelled with fictional sample data. Existing product screenshots remain original application views in the gallery below. Software availability distinctions are retained.
- These are updates to the private TOTUS Site. The source PANI website and underlying software applications are not changed by this repository.

Validation: German/English PANI and Tracker pages, both homepages and an unaffected Invest page checked on desktop.
