PANI Property Management

PANI Property Management (PANI Hausverwaltung) is the software behind our own property management operation: rental and HOA management, accounting, AI inbox, and portals. Built for the German legal framework.

Modules

Seven modules, one management platform.

Built for the property management team. Tenants, owners, service providers, and the advisory board work through their own portals.

Rental management

Leases, rent increases with rent index checks, deposits, terminations, rent roll.

HOA management (WEG)

Owners’ meetings, resolution records, annual budget, HOA fee statements, special assessments, advisory board.

Accounting & payments

Ledger, SEPA direct debit via EBICS, bank reconciliation, dunning, DATEV export.

Service charges & energy

Service charge statements, heating costs, CO2 cost allocation, meters, GEG checks.

AI inbox & cases

AI classifies and routes incoming email and creates cases automatically.

Portals

Tenant and owner portals live; access for service providers and the advisory board too.

Documents & compliance

PDF pipeline, versioning, owner reports, compliance cockpit with Section 147 AO and GwG KYC.

Workflow

From onboarding to reporting.

Four steps cover the full day-to-day of property management.

Onboarding

Set up properties, units, and leases with a wizard.

Daily operations

AI sorts the inbox; rent debits and payments run automatically.

Statements

Service charge and HOA fee statements, including PDF generation.

Reporting & portals

Owner reports and portals keep everyone involved informed.
