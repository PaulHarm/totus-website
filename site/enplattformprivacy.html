02

Scope & general information.

This privacy policy applies to the website totus.info, the TOTUS platform at app.totus.info, and all associated communication channels (email, phone, demo requests, career applications).

We process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide our services, respond to inquiries, or comply with legal requirements. Processing is based exclusively on legal provisions (GDPR, BDSG, TDDDG).

03 · Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO

Server log files.

When you visit our website, technical information is automatically recorded that your browser transmits to our server:

  • IP address (anonymized after 7 days)
  • Browser type and version, operating system
  • Referrer URL and accessed URL
  • Time of access

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in security, stability, and functionality of our services). This data is automatically deleted after 30 days.

04 · § 25 TDDDG

Cookies & local storage.

Our website uses exclusively technically necessary cookies and local storage. These include:

  • Language preference (DE/EN toggle, local storage)
  • Authentication token for logged-in users (HttpOnly cookie, only for app.totus.info)
  • CSRF protection token (session cookie)

Tracking- oder Werbe-Cookies setzen wir nicht ein. Es gibt daher keine Notwendigkeit eines Cookie-Banners mit Opt-In nach § 25 Abs. 2 TDDDG.

Sie können Cookies in Ihrem Browser jederzeit löschen oder deren Setzung einschränken. Bei eingeschränkten Cookies kann es zu Funktionseinschränkungen bei der Anmeldung kommen.

05 · Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b, f DSGVO

Contact & demo requests.

When you contact us by email, phone, or via the demo form, we process the following data:

  • Name, business email, company, role
  • Company type, primary interest
  • Content of your message and attached context about current setup
  • Timestamp and IP address at the time of inquiry

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures at your request) and (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your inquiry).

The data is stored in our CRM and deleted after processing, at the latest after 24 months of inactivity, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

06 · § 26 BDSG

Career applications.

When you apply for a position with us, we process the data submitted with your application (name, contact details, CV, cover letter, references) exclusively for the purpose of the application process.

Legal basis: § 26 (1) BDSG (initiation of an employment relationship). If an employment relationship is established, the data is transferred to our personnel file. If rejected, application documents are deleted after 6 months.

With your application you can consent to inclusion in our Talent Pool, we then store your documents for 24 months to potentially approach you for suitable future positions.

07 · Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO

Use of the TOTUS platform.

As a Software-as-a-Service provider, we process data of TOTUS platform users in the context of a concluded service agreement. This includes:

  • Master data of users (name, email, role, company)
  • Authentication and session data (incl. SSO integration)
  • Platform usage data (access times, accessed modules, audit logs)
  • Investment data and deal information that the customer feeds into the platform

For platform users of our business customers, the respective customer is the data protection controller. We act as processor per Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of a concluded Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

09 · Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO

AI processing with Claude (Anthropic).

For AI-powered features in the platform, such as automated deal assessments, IC memo generation, and meeting notes, we use the Claude API by Anthropic Ireland Limited (Dublin, EU).

Inputs to AI features are transmitted to Anthropic encrypted and processed there, exclusively within the EU. No training on customer data takes place, contractually guaranteed with Anthropic. Data is deleted after processing per Anthropic retention periods.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (contract performance) for platform users. Processing per Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of a concluded DPA with Anthropic Ireland.

10 · Art. 5 Abs. 1 lit. e DSGVO

Retention.

We store personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the respective purpose or as statutory retention obligations require. In detail:

  • Server logs: 30 days (IP anonymization after 7 days)
  • Demo requests / CRM data: 24 months after last contact
  • Rejected applications: 6 months
  • Applications (Talent Pool): 24 months with consent
  • Contract & invoice data: 10 years (§ 147 AO)
  • Platform data (customers): During contract term + 30-day backup cycle
